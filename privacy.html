Strait Command is designed to play without an account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or a developer-operated server.

Who provides the app

Strait Command is developed and published by Dayton Thorpe under the Good Little Apps brand. Good Little Apps is a public-facing developer brand, not a separate data controller.

Summary

Strait Command does not collect data from the app. Campaign progress, settings, and statistics are stored locally in the app's container on your device. The developer cannot access those records.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the minimum state needed to resume a campaign and remember local preferences and results. This includes the selected scenario, deterministic campaign seed, watch state, orders, crisis decisions, branch readiness, sector status, statistics, and haptics preference.

No accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking

The app has no account or login, developer API, cloud synchronization, remote configuration, advertising SDK, attribution SDK, analytics, telemetry, session replay, or third-party crash-reporting SDK. It does not use data from other companies' apps or websites to track you.

Apple services and device backups

Apple processes App Store downloads and may include app data in an iOS or iPadOS device backup according to your Apple settings. These are Apple services, not collection by Dayton Thorpe or Good Little Apps. Review Apple's privacy information and your device settings for details.

Public website

This product, support, and privacy website contains no developer-authored analytics, advertising, account system, or contact form. Its hosting and network providers may process ordinary request and security information needed to deliver the pages under their own terms. Visiting the website is separate from playing the app.

Voluntary email

If you email goodlittleapps.support@gmail.com, the email service and developer receive the address, message, headers, and attachments you choose to send. Use this contact for a defect report, product feedback, or a privacy or legal notice. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Your choices and deletion

You can remove campaigns, statistics, and preferences from Settings using Reset All Progress. Deleting Strait Command from the device removes its local app container, subject to any device backup you control. Because the developer does not receive gameplay data, there is no developer-held gameplay record to retrieve, correct, or delete.

Children

Strait Command is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Fictional scenario

Strait Command is a fictional conflict simulation. It is not a prediction, operational guidance, government service, or endorsement of real-world violence.

Policy changes

If a future version changes the app's data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released. The effective date above identifies this policy version.

Contact

For a privacy or legal notice about Strait Command, email goodlittleapps.support@gmail.com.
