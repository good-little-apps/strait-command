Status guide

What the strategic meters mean

Command points

The orders available this watch. Unspent points do not replace damaged forces or lost time.

Readiness

The condition of air, missile, and logistics branches. Strikes can reduce the strength or availability of their orders.

Capital integrity

The capital's ability to withstand attack. If it reaches zero, the campaign is lost.

National resolve

The political and civil capacity to continue the defense. Crisis decisions and battlefield outcomes can move it sharply.

Enemy lift

The shipping capacity still moving through the strait. Interdiction reduces what can reach contested sectors.

Partner assets

Three powerful one-shot capabilities that unlock over time. Their timing matters as much as their effect.

Common questions

Campaigns and troubleshooting

How is progress saved?

The app saves the active campaign after accepted orders and other state changes. Leave the campaign at any time, then choose Continue Campaign from the main screen.

Why is an order unavailable?

Open its order sheet. The disabled option explains the exact command-point, readiness, timing, or game-state requirement.

Why can't I end the watch?

An active crisis must be resolved first. Choose either crisis response; crisis decisions do not cost command points.

What is Daily Strait?

It is a campaign generated entirely on-device from the current UTC date. The same date and app version create the same starting scenario without contacting a server.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Campaigns, Daily Strait, saves, statistics, and settings work without a Good Little Apps account or an internet connection.

How do I erase my data?

Open Settings, choose Reset All Progress, and confirm. Deleting the app also removes its local app container, subject to any device backup you control.

What accessibility support is included?

Sea lanes, coastal sectors, meters, order choices, reports, and key controls include VoiceOver labels, values, and hints. The interface supports iPhone and iPad in portrait and landscape.
